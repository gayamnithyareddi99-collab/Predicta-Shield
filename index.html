How Predicta Shield Works

Predicta Shield transforms cybersecurity from a complex technical function into a simple, real-time operational system by combining behavioural intelligence, explainable AI, and continuous compliance automation.

01

Continuous Activity Monitoring

The platform observes daily workflows such as file handling, logins, and system access across devices and cloud environments. Instead of intrusive scanning, it uses lightweight monitoring to understand how work is actually performed in real time.

02

Behaviour & Risk Analysis

AI models analyse behavioural patterns to detect risky actions and vulnerabilities. By identifying patterns linked to human error, the system predicts potential issues before they turn into security incidents.

03

Explainable Guidance (XAI)

Instead of technical alerts, Predicta Shield provides clear, plain-language instructions that explain what the risk is, why it matters, and exactly what action to take enabling non-technical users to respond instantly.

04

Compliance & Audit Automation

All actions, risks, and fixes are automatically recorded and structured into audit-ready evidence. This ensures continuous compliance with regulations and removes the need for manual documentation or last-minute audit preparation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand how Predicta Shield simplifies cybersecurity, compliance, and risk management through AI-driven, explainable guidance for modern businesses.

What is Predicta Shield?

Predicta Shield is an AI-powered cybersecurity and compliance platform designed to protect businesses through real-time behavioural risk detection and explainable guidance. It transforms complex technical threats into clear, actionable steps for non-technical users.

How is Predicta Shield different from traditional security tools?

Unlike traditional tools that generate technical alerts, Predicta Shield uses Explainable AI (XAI) to translate risks into plain-language actions. This removes dependency on IT specialists and enables teams to respond instantly within their daily workflows.

How does Predicta Shield detect risks?

The platform continuously monitors workflow behaviour such as login patterns, file handling, and system activity. It identifies risky patterns and predicts potential issues before they become security incidents.

Does it support compliance and audits?

Yes. Predicta Shield automatically aligns daily operations with regulatory requirements like UK GDPR and Cyber Essentials. It also generates audit-ready evidence, eliminating manual documentation efforts.

Who is Predicta Shield designed for?

The platform is built for SMEs, operational teams, and non-technical staff across industries such as healthcare, finance, retail, and professional services.

How does this improve business value?

Predicta Shield reduces risk, lowers operational costs, and improves compliance readiness. By preventing incidents before they occur, businesses can operate more securely and efficiently without relying on expensive consultants.
